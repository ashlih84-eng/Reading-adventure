# Mastery System

Every attempt is retained. Passing a mission can award its one-time reward once; replay adds evidence without farming stars. Low later scores do not delete earlier success. Region 2 requires all five lessons, review, villain challenge, checkpoint, at least five mastered skills, and eight independent successful missions.

Intervention signals come from low-scoring skills and unclear-word history. Later work can route these signals to shorter passages, listen-then-read, extra vocabulary, or supporting lessons.
